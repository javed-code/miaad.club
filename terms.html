What Miaad is

Miaad shows your credit cards and bills in one place and reminds you of due dates. It isn’t a bank. It doesn’t lend money, offer instalment plans or hold your money, and it doesn’t give financial advice.

Paying your cards and bills is still up to you

You can stop it at any time by taking away the permission in your phone’s settings. The privacy policy has the details.
