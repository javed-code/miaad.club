Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Miaad (ميعاد) shows people who live in the UAE all their credit cards and bills in one place. It’s run by Miaad, Mohamed Bin Zayed City, MBZ 19, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates (“Miaad”, “we”). This policy explains what we collect, why, where it’s kept and the choices you have. It covers the Miaad apps and this website.

Miaad is in a private test with invited people, so some features described here may change. When they do, we’ll update this page.

What we collect

When you sign in

  • Your mobile number, to send you a sign-in code and to recognise you.
  • The name and type of each device you sign in on, so sessions can be seen and ended.
  • Invite codes you use or share.

Your cards and bills

  • For each card: the bank, the last four digits, a name you give it and its credit limit. Never the full card number: the app won’t accept one.
  • For each bill: the statement and due dates, the total and minimum due, and whether you’ve marked it paid.
  • Cards can be from banks in the UAE or, starting with India, from abroad. A card’s bills stay in its own currency.

If you connect your email (optional)

  • With your permission, Miaad gets read-only access to your Gmail or Outlook mailbox.
  • We only look for statement and payment-confirmation emails from our list of banks’ own email domains, and only open those your email provider confirms really came from the bank. We don’t read your other emails.
  • From those emails and their PDF statements we take the bill summary: the card’s last four digits, the statement and due dates, and the amounts due. From a payment confirmation we take the card’s last four digits, the amount paid and the date, to show the bill as paid. We don’t keep the emails or their attachments. To read each email only once, we keep a fingerprint of its ID, the bank and the result.
  • We keep the access token Google or Microsoft gives us, encrypted, and delete it when you disconnect.

Your details for opening statements

Banks lock statement PDFs with a password made from personal details, such as the first letters of your name and your date of birth. If you give us your name (in English letters) and date of birth, we use them to open your statements automatically. We work out each password when a statement arrives and never store it.

Consents

A record of each consent you give or withdraw, for example to the terms or to email access, with the date and the version of the wording.

This website

No accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

How we use it

  • To run Miaad: show your cards and what’s due, remind you of due dates, and help you avoid interest and late fees.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Google user data. Miaad’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Gmail data only to show you your own cards and bills, never for ads or offers. People at Miaad don’t read it, except when you ask us to (for example, to fix a problem), for security, or when the law requires it.

Reading statements with AI

Our own code reads most statements. When it can’t, we send the statement’s text to Anthropic’s Claude model to read the bill summary. Before anything is sent, we remove your name, card numbers, account numbers, Emirates ID, phone numbers and email addresses, and on statements from India, PAN and Aadhaar numbers. Anthropic processes this masked text in the United States under its commercial terms, which don’t allow it to use our data to train its models.

Where it’s kept

Your data is stored in the UAE: on Oracle Cloud’s UAE Central region in Abu Dhabi during the private test, then on Microsoft Azure’s UAE North region in Dubai. The exception is the masked statement text that Anthropic reads in the United States.

Our WhatsApp number uses WhatsApp’s UAE local storage, so the content of our WhatsApp messages is stored only in the UAE, although Meta may process it outside the UAE for up to 60 minutes while delivering it.

How long we keep it

During the private test we keep your data until the test ends, then delete it, unless you choose to stay on as a member. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect your email at any time in the app. For Gmail, disconnecting also withdraws Miaad’s access in your Google account.
  • Remove any card in the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it and your account: write to hello@miaad.club. We reply within 30 days.
  • Under the UAE’s Personal Data Protection Law you can also object to how we use your data and complain to the UAE Data Office.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Email access tokens are encrypted again with our own key.
  • We never store full card numbers or statement passwords.
  • Only masked text ever reaches an AI model.

Children

Miaad is for adults, 18 and over, who live in the UAE.

Changes

When this policy changes we’ll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app about important changes.
