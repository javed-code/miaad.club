Halal by design

Miaad never lends money or offers buy now, pay later or instalments. Credit cards are the one exception, because a card paid in full and on time costs no interest. So Miaad makes paying in full the default.

  • Autopay pays the full statement balance by default
  • A clear warning before you pay only the minimum
  • No draws, spins or mystery boxes

Your data stays yours

Read-only email, only from your banks

If you connect your email, Miaad reads only the statements your banks send. It never sends, changes or deletes anything.

No card numbers, no passwords

Miaad keeps only the last four digits of a card, and never stores your statement passwords.

Kept in the UAE

Your data is stored in Microsoft Azure’s UAE North region, in Dubai.

Your choice, always

Disconnect your email, remove a card or delete your data whenever you like.
