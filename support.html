Help and contact

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Write to hello@miaad.club and a person will reply within two working days. You can also call +971 58 884 0928.

How do I disconnect my email?

In the app, tap your Gmail or Outlook mailbox, then Disconnect. Miaad stops reading it straight away and deletes its access. Cards already added stay until you remove them.

How do I delete my data?

Write to hello@miaad.club from the email address you use with us, or tell us the mobile number you signed in with. We’ll confirm it’s you, delete your account and everything in it within 30 days, and let you know when it’s done.

Can Miaad see my card number?

No. Miaad only keeps the last four digits, and the app won’t accept a full card number.

Does Miaad store my statement passwords?

No. When a statement arrives, Miaad works out its password from your name and date of birth, opens it, and forgets the password.

Can I add a credit card from outside the UAE?

Yes, cards from India. They have their own section, with their bills in rupees, kept apart from your dirham total, and the same reminders. For now, you pay them in your bank’s app; we’re working on a shortcut to pay them in your UPI app. More countries will follow, one at a time, after we’ve checked each country’s rules.

Is Miaad free?

Yes, during the private test.

Why is Miaad invite-only?

We’re testing it with a small group first. To join the waitlist, write to hello@miaad.club.
